Privacy Policy

ExpiryMate for Android is built to work entirely on your phone or tablet. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Applies to the ExpiryMate Android app and its home screen widget. Using an iPhone? See the iOS Privacy Policy.

The short version

  • Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. ExpiryMate has no account system and no server.
  • The developer does not receive, collect, or have access to the items, notes, photos, or other content you enter.
  • ExpiryMate contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not sell or share personal information.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. ExpiryMate never sees your payment details.
  • Google's Play Billing Library, which the app uses for the optional subscription, sends technical diagnostic information about billing to Google. It does not include anything you enter in the app.

1. Information you enter

You choose what to add. Depending on how you use ExpiryMate, the app may store:

  • Tracked items: item name, category, expiration date, optional start date, optional notes, reminder choices, repeat schedule, archive status, and renewal history.
  • Photos you choose to attach to an item.
  • Custom categories you create (name, icon, and color).
  • Preferences: an optional first name used only for the greeting on the Home screen, appearance (system/light/dark), default reminder time, and the "Expiring Soon" threshold.

Please avoid entering highly sensitive information, such as full document numbers or passwords, in item names or notes. Item names can appear in notifications and in the widget, which may be visible on your lock screen or home screen.

2. How information is stored

ExpiryMate stores your items in an on-device database inside the app's private storage. Photos are saved as image files in the same private storage, and preferences are stored in the app's local settings. Other apps cannot read this storage; Android's app sandbox and device encryption protect it.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include ExpiryMate's items, photos, and settings in your device backup and in device-to-device transfers. On Android 9 and later, ExpiryMate allows this only for cloud backups that are end-to-end encrypted with your device's screen lock; on Android 8, Android's standard backup applies. Backups are created and managed by Android and Google according to your settings; the developer has no access to them. ExpiryMate does not use any cloud sync of its own.

3. Does anything leave your device?

ExpiryMate does not transmit the content you enter to the developer or to any server. The app's own code makes no network requests. Information leaves your device only in these cases, which are controlled by you or by Google:

  • Export (Pro): when you choose Export Data, the app creates a JSON file of your items (photos are not included) and you choose where to save it. After that, the file is handled by the location you selected.
  • Email support: if you contact us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. The pre-filled support email adds only the app version and Android version.
  • Google Play services: subscription purchases, restores, and ratings are handled by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. Device backups follow your Android and Google account settings.
  • Google Play Billing diagnostics: Google's Play Billing Library, which is part of the app, sends technical diagnostic logs about its billing operations to Google, together with device details: device model and manufacturer, Android version, language and region settings, mobile network code, time zone offset, network type, and the app version. Google processes them under the Google Privacy Policy. They contain no items, notes, photos, or other content you enter, and the developer does not receive them.

In Google Play's Data safety section, ExpiryMate therefore declares one data type as collected: App info and performance (diagnostics), sent by the Google Play Billing Library, not shared with third parties, with the purpose "Analytics". No other user data is collected or shared.

4. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled on your device and are not sent through any server or push notification service. On Android 13 and later, ExpiryMate asks for permission to send notifications so it can remind you before items expire. You can allow or deny this, and change it anytime in Android Settings > Apps > ExpiryMate > Notifications. If notifications are off, the app keeps working; you just won't receive reminders.

A notification shows the item's name and its expiration date, and may appear on your lock screen depending on your Android notification settings.

5. Photos

When you attach a photo, ExpiryMate uses the Android photo picker, which gives the app only the image you select. The app does not request access to your photo library or storage and does not use the camera. A resized copy of the selected image is stored in the app's private storage and is deleted when you delete the item or replace the photo.

6. Home screen widget

The widget is drawn by ExpiryMate itself from its on-device database. It shows each active item's name, category, and expiration date, plus a status summary. Notes, photos, and renewal history are never shown in the widget. Nothing is sent off your device to display it. Widget content may be visible to anyone who can see your home screen.

7. Subscriptions

ExpiryMate Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play. Payments, billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Google Play. ExpiryMate uses the Google Play Billing Library on your device to read a signed confirmation of whether your subscription is active, and keeps that status on the device so Pro keeps working offline for up to 7 days after the last successful check with Google Play. ExpiryMate does not receive your name, email address, or payment information, and does not operate a server that stores purchase records.

8. Third-party services

ExpiryMate does not include any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking software and has no server of its own. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not sell or share personal information.

The app is built with Android Jetpack libraries that run on your device, and with Google's Play Billing Library, which talks to the Google Play Store app on your device to process subscriptions and sends the billing diagnostics described in section 3 to Google. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses when you visit it, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers.

9. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): to show your expiration reminders.
  • Run at startup (receive boot completed): Android clears scheduled reminders when the device restarts, so ExpiryMate reschedules them after a restart.
  • Google Play billing: added by the Play Billing Library to offer the optional subscription.
  • Internet and network state: added by the Play Billing Library's diagnostics component (network state is also used by Android Jetpack WorkManager). ExpiryMate's own code makes no network requests.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by Android Jetpack WorkManager, which keeps the home screen widget up to date.

ExpiryMate does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

10. Your control and data deletion

  • Edit or delete an item: open the item and use the ⋮ menu, or touch and hold an item in All Items. Deleting an item also deletes its photo, renewal history, and scheduled reminders.
  • Archive: archived items stay on your device and can be restored or deleted later.
  • Remove everything: uninstall ExpiryMate, or use Android Settings > Apps > ExpiryMate > Storage > Clear storage. This removes the app's data from your device. Copies in existing device backups follow your Google account's backup settings.
  • Notifications: manage or turn them off anytime in Android Settings.

Because the developer does not hold your data, we cannot view, recover, or delete it for you, and we cannot restore it if it is lost.

11. Security

Your data is protected by Android security features, including the app sandbox and device encryption. Keeping a screen lock on your device and keeping Android updated helps keep your data safe.

12. Children's privacy

ExpiryMate is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), because the app does not send the information you enter anywhere and the developer receives no personal information from it. Purchases made by minors are subject to Google Play's parental controls and Family Link settings.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Since the developer does not receive your information, you can exercise these rights directly in the app (by editing or deleting your items) or by uninstalling it. For information held by Google, including Google Play Billing diagnostics, see the Google Privacy Policy. If we receive a support email from you, you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

14. Changes to this policy

If ExpiryMate's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes before they take effect.
