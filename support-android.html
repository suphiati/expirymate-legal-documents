Adding and managing tracked items

How do I add an item?

Tap the green + button on the Home screen (or the + in All Items). Enter a name, choose a category, pick the expiration date, and optionally add a start date, reminders, a photo, and notes. Tap Save Item.

What do the status colors mean?
  • Active (green): more than your "Expiring Soon" threshold away (30 days by default).
  • Expiring Soon (amber): within the threshold, including "Expires Today".
  • Expired (red): the expiration date has passed.
  • Archived (gray): items you've put away. They don't count toward the Free limit.
How do I edit, renew, archive, or delete an item?
  • Edit: open the item and tap ⋮ > Edit.
  • Renew: open the item and tap Mark as Renewed, then choose the new expiration date. The previous date is kept in the item's renewal history and reminders are rescheduled.
  • Archive: tap Archive on the item, or touch and hold it in All Items and choose Archive. Use the Archived filter to find it again and choose Unarchive.
  • Delete: touch and hold the item in All Items and choose Delete, or tap ⋮ > Delete on the item. You'll be asked to confirm. Deleting is permanent.
How do I find an item?

In All Items, use the search field (it searches names, notes, and category names) and the status chips: All, Active, Expiring Soon, Expired, and Archived. Pro adds category filters. The Calendar tab shows expirations by day.

Notifications and reminders

How do reminders work?

For each item you can choose when to be reminded: 30, 14, 7, or 1 day before, or on the day it expires. Pro also lets you pick custom days and add several reminders per item. Reminders arrive at your Reminder Time (Settings > Notifications). They are local notifications, so they work offline. To save battery, Android may deliver a reminder up to about an hour after the chosen time.

I'm not getting reminders. What should I check?
  1. In ExpiryMate, open Settings. If it says notifications are off, tap Open Settings and allow notifications for ExpiryMate.
  2. In Android Settings > Apps > ExpiryMate > Notifications, make sure Expiration reminders is turned on.
  3. Check that Do Not Disturb or a bedtime mode isn't silencing notifications.
  4. If your phone has aggressive battery saving for apps, set ExpiryMate's battery usage to Unrestricted or Optimized (not Restricted).
  5. Open the item and confirm it has at least one reminder selected and that the reminder date isn't already in the past. Reminders whose time has passed are not scheduled.
  6. Make sure your device's date, time, and time zone are set correctly. Archived items don't send reminders.
ExpiryMate keeps the soonest 60 reminders scheduled and tops up the rest automatically as they fire and each time you open the app. If you force-stop the app, Android cancels its reminders until you open it again.
I upgraded or my Pro subscription ended. What happens to my reminders?

With Pro, every reminder you choose is scheduled. On the Free plan, each item keeps 1 scheduled reminder (the earliest one you selected). Your reminder choices are saved, so they come back if you subscribe again.

Home screen widget

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen, tap Widgets, find ExpiryMate, and add the Expirations widget. Resize it to switch between the small, medium, and large layouts. The widget is available on both the Free and Pro plans. Tap an item in the widget to open it in the app.

The widget is empty, out of date, or says "Open ExpiryMate".
  1. Open ExpiryMate once and finish the welcome screens. The app updates the widget whenever you add, edit, renew, archive, or delete an item.
  2. The widget only shows active (non-archived) items. If you have none, it invites you to add one.
  3. Countdowns update after midnight, the next time your device wakes up.
  4. If it still looks wrong, remove the widget and add it again.

The widget shows item names, categories, and dates. It never shows notes or photos.

Subscriptions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In ExpiryMate, go to Settings > Manage Subscription, or open the Google Play Store app and tap your profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > ExpiryMate Pro. From there you can switch plans or cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?
  1. Make sure the Google Play Store is signed in with the Google account you used to subscribe.
  2. Open ExpiryMate. Pro is detected automatically; you can also tap Settings > Restore Purchases (also on the Pro screen).
  3. If no active subscription is found, you'll see a message saying so for this Google account.

Restoring unlocks Pro. It does not transfer your items, which are stored on the device (see below for moving devices).

How do I get a refund?

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play; see Google Play's refund help. You can also email us with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with "GPA.") and we will review it. Never send card details.

My payment failed. Will I lose Pro?

If Google Play can't charge your payment method at renewal, it may keep Pro active for a short grace period while it retries. Update your payment method in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. If the subscription lapses, your data stays on your device and Free plan limits apply.

Does Pro work offline?

Yes. ExpiryMate remembers your active subscription on the device, so Pro keeps working without a connection. It needs to confirm the subscription with Google Play at least once every 7 days; if your device stays offline longer, Pro features pause until it can check again. Your items are never affected.

I subscribed but Pro isn't unlocked.
  1. If the payment is pending (for example a cash or bank payment, or a family approval), Pro unlocks once it completes.
  2. Tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
  3. Check that the Play Store is signed in with the Google account you subscribed with.
  4. Still stuck? Email us with your order number. Never send card details.

Data storage, moving devices, and deletion

Where is my data stored? Is there an account or cloud sync?

Everything is stored on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync, and the developer cannot see your data. Android may include ExpiryMate's data in your Google account backup, depending on your settings. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I move my items to a new phone (including from an iPhone)?

Restoring a new Android phone from your Google backup, or copying data during setup, brings ExpiryMate's data with it when Android backup is on. Pro subscribers can also use Settings > Export Data to save a JSON file and Import Data on the new device. The export format is the same on iPhone and Android, so you can move your items between them. Photos are not included in exports, and importing skips items that already exist.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items in the app (this also deletes their photos, renewal history, and reminders). To remove everything, uninstall ExpiryMate or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > ExpiryMate > Storage. Because your data isn't stored by the developer, it can't be recovered after deletion unless you have a backup or export.
