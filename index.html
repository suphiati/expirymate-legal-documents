Legal & Support

Never miss an important expiration date.

ExpiryMate is an iPhone app that tracks passports, insurance, warranties, memberships, medicine, and anything else with a date, and reminds you before it expires. Your records stay on your device.

ExpiryMate at a glance

  • No account, no sign-in, and no server. Your items are stored on your iPhone.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Optional ExpiryMate Pro subscription, billed and managed by Apple.
