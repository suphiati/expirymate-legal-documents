Privacy Policy

ExpiryMate is built to work entirely on your iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · Applies to the ExpiryMate iOS app and its Home Screen widget.

The short version

  • Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. ExpiryMate has no account system, and the developer runs no server.
  • The developer does not receive, collect, or have access to the items, notes, photos, or other content you enter.
  • ExpiryMate contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and does not sell or share personal information.
  • Subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. Starting with version 1.1, ExpiryMate uses RevenueCat to confirm whether ExpiryMate Pro is active, which receives your App Store purchase records for ExpiryMate under a random ID that is not linked to your identity (see Subscriptions). ExpiryMate never sees your payment details.

1. Information you enter

You choose what to add. Depending on how you use ExpiryMate, the app may store:

  • Tracked items: item name, category, expiration date, optional start date, optional notes, reminder choices, repeat schedule, archive status, and renewal history.
  • Photos you choose to attach to an item.
  • Custom categories you create (name, icon, and color).
  • Preferences: an optional first name used only for the greeting on the Home screen, appearance (light/dark), default reminder time, and the "Expiring Soon" threshold.

Please avoid entering highly sensitive information, such as full document numbers or passwords, in item names or notes. Item names can appear in notifications and in the widget, which may be visible on your Lock Screen or Home Screen.

2. How information is stored

ExpiryMate stores your items in an on-device database (Apple's SwiftData). Photos are saved as image files in the app's private storage. Preferences are stored in the app's local settings. This information is protected by iOS app sandboxing and data protection.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include ExpiryMate's data in those backups. Backups are created and managed by Apple and your device according to your settings; the developer has no access to them. ExpiryMate does not use iCloud sync.

3. Does anything leave your device?

ExpiryMate does not transmit the content you enter to the developer or to any server. Information leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Export (Pro): when you choose Export Data, the app creates a JSON file of your items (photos are not included) and you choose where to save or share it. After that, the file is handled by the destination you selected.
  • Email support: if you contact us, we receive the email address and any information you include in your message. The pre-filled support email includes the app version and iOS version only.
  • Apple services: App Store purchases, restores, and ratings are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Device backups follow your iOS settings.
  • Subscription status (version 1.1 and later): the app sends your App Store purchase records for ExpiryMate and limited technical information to RevenueCat, as described in Subscriptions. Your items, notes, and photos are never included.

For App Store purposes, ExpiryMate's privacy label lists the subscription data described in section 7 under "Data Not Linked to You". It is not used for tracking.

4. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled by iOS on your device and are not sent through any server or push notification service. ExpiryMate asks for notification permission so it can remind you before items expire. You can allow or deny this, and change it anytime in iOS Settings > Notifications > ExpiryMate. If notifications are off, the app keeps working; you just won't receive reminders.

A notification shows the item's name and its expiration date, and the item's photo if it has one. It may appear on your Lock Screen depending on your iOS notification settings, including the notification preview setting.

5. Photos

When you attach a photo, ExpiryMate uses Apple's system photo picker, which gives the app only the image you select. The app does not request access to your full photo library and does not use the camera. A resized copy of the selected image is stored in the app's private storage and is deleted when you delete the item or replace the photo. To show the photo in an item's reminders, the app gives iOS a temporary copy for each scheduled reminder. These copies stay on your device. Copies for upcoming reminders are replaced whenever reminders are rescheduled, and reminders that have already appeared keep the photo in Notification Center until you clear them, delete the item, or change its photo.

6. Home Screen widget

To show upcoming expirations in the widget, the app writes a small file to an App Group container on your device that only ExpiryMate and its widget can read. The file contains each active item's identifier, name, category name and icon, and expiration date, plus your "Expiring Soon" threshold. Notes, photos, and renewal history are never shared with the widget. This file stays on your device. Widget content may be visible on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

7. Subscriptions

ExpiryMate Pro is optional and sold through the App Store as an auto-renewable subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments, billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple. ExpiryMate does not receive your name, email address, or payment information from Apple, and the developer does not operate a server that stores purchase records.

Starting with version 1.1, ExpiryMate uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription management service, to show the Pro plans, process purchases and restores through the App Store, and confirm whether ExpiryMate Pro is active. The app also still reads Apple's signed purchase confirmation on your device with StoreKit, so Pro keeps working offline. When the app starts or returns to the foreground, when you buy Pro, and when you restore purchases, the RevenueCat software in the app sends RevenueCat:

  • a random identifier created by the app on your device (it is not your name, email address, or Apple Account);
  • your App Store purchase records for ExpiryMate, such as the product, purchase and expiration dates, transaction identifiers, price, currency, and App Store country;
  • technical information: app version and bundle identifier, iOS version, device model, preferred languages, App Store country, whether the app is a test or App Store installation, an identifier Apple provides to ExpiryMate's developer for this device (identifier for vendor), and your IP address, which every internet connection reveals.

RevenueCat uses this information to validate purchases, prevent fraud, restore subscriptions, and give the developer aggregated subscription reports. It is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising or tracking, and not sold. Your items, notes, photos, and preferences are never sent. RevenueCat processes this information as a service provider, mainly in the United States, under the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. To ask for your subscription record to be deleted, email us with the order details from your App Store receipt so we can find it.

8. Third-party services

Apart from RevenueCat for subscriptions (see Subscriptions), ExpiryMate does not include any third-party software or use third-party servers. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking software, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share personal information.

Apart from the RevenueCat software, the app relies only on Apple system frameworks (such as SwiftData, UserNotifications, WidgetKit, PhotosUI, and StoreKit) that run on your device. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses when you visit it, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers.

9. Your control and data deletion

  • Edit or delete an item: open the item and use Edit or Delete, or swipe left on an item in All Items. Deleting an item also deletes its photo, renewal history, and scheduled reminders.
  • Archive: archived items stay on your device and can be restored or deleted later.
  • Remove everything: delete the ExpiryMate app from your iPhone. This removes the app's data from your device, including the widget data. Copies in existing device backups follow Apple's backup settings.
  • Notifications: manage or turn them off anytime in iOS Settings.

Because the developer does not hold your data, we cannot view, recover, or delete it for you, and we cannot restore it if it is lost.

10. Security

Your data is protected by iOS security features, including app sandboxing and data protection, which encrypts app files when your device is locked with a passcode. Keeping your device passcode-protected and updated helps keep your data safe.

11. Children's privacy

ExpiryMate is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The only information the app sends is the subscription data described in Subscriptions, which is not linked to anyone's identity. Purchases made by minors are subject to Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy settings.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Your items never leave your device, so you can exercise these rights directly in the app (by editing or deleting your items) or by deleting the app. For the subscription records held by RevenueCat (see Subscriptions), email us and we will handle your request. For information held by Apple, see Apple's Privacy Policy. If we receive a support email from you, you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

13. Changes to this policy

If ExpiryMate's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes before they take effect.
